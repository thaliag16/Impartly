Issue 2: An invented fund closes

Meadowbank Capital, an invented firm, closed its third fund at $310 million, above its $250 million target, according to an invented filing seen this week.

The fund will back invented seed-stage startups in logistics and climate, with checks of $1 million to $4 million.

Limited partners include two invented pension plans and an invented university endowment, which together committed about half of the total.

The firm said it expects to make roughly 30 investments over four years, keeping a reserve for follow-on rounds in its strongest companies.
