An invented fund closes its third vehicle

Meadowbank Capital, an invented venture firm, closed its third fund at $310 million, above its $250 million target, according to an invented filing seen this week by The Invented Brief.

The fund will back invented seed-stage startups in logistics and climate software, writing first checks of $1 million to $4 million and keeping about half of the fund for follow-on rounds.

Limited partners include two invented pension plans and an invented university endowment, which together committed about half of the total, the filing shows.
